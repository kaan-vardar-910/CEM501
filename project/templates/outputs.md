  Question / Description of Discrepancy
  There is a conflict between the contract drawings and the technical specifications regarding the maximum allowable spacing for pipe  
  hangers.
   - Drawing M-401 indicates a maximum hanger spacing of 10 feet.
   - Specification Section 23 05 13 requires a maximum spacing of 8 feet.

  Please clarify which requirement shall govern the installation.

  Schedule Impact
   - Activity: Overhead Rough-in
   - Start Date: March 10

  Cost Impact
   - Nature: TBD but anticipated. If the 8-foot spacing requirement is enforced, there will be an increase in material (hangers) and   
     labor costs compared to the 10-foot spacing shown on the drawings.


  Suggested Resolution
  We recommend adhering to the more stringent requirement of 8-foot maximum spacing as per Specification Section 23 05 13 to ensure    
  long-term structural integrity and compliance with technical standards. Please confirm if this is acceptable or if the 10-foot       
  spacing on Drawing M-401 was intentional for this specific area.
